# Madhuri / Mahadevi: Nandani Math vs Vantara

Research note for the reel. Two names, one elephant: **Mahadevi** in the court
papers and PETA filings, **Madhuri** in Kolhapur. Female, around 36 years old.

Research limitation: petaindia.com, barandbench.com, thelogicalindian.com,
wikipedia and newsonair were blocked by this environment's network proxy. Their
content below comes from search-result summaries, not the pages themselves.

## Side by side

| | Nandani Math, Kolhapur | Vantara, Jamnagar |
|---|---|---|
| **Duration** | 1992 to July 2025, about **33 years**. Brought in at age 3. | 30 July 2025 to 11 Sep 2026, **409 days** (about 13 months). |
| **Space** | One shed. The **same concrete slab** for 33 years. | Vantara is about **3,500 acres**. The elephant facility is reported at about **998 acres** (some sources say a 600-acre elephant habitat). |
| **Company** | Solitary. No other elephants. | **200+ rescued elephants.** She was given supervised contact and formed bonds with other elephants, per Vantara. |
| **Restraint** | Chained. PETA alleges she was controlled with weapons. | Not chained, per Vantara. |
| **Surface** | Concrete. | Natural substrate, plus daily water access. |
| **Use** | Processions, village begging, and **rented out for Muharram and other events**, per PETA. | None. Medical rehabilitation only. |
| **Medical** | Grade 4 arthritis, foot rot, overgrown toenails, worn-out footpads, laminitis, a long-standing toenail abscess, swelling in both knees, and radiographs showing **fractures and arthritis in her front feet**. | 25,000 sq ft elephant hospital. **Hydrotherapy** (buoyancy unloads the inflamed joints, daily water access for hours), laser therapy, targeted nutrition, specialised foot care. MRI and CT on site. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy. A multidisciplinary team of vets, caregivers, nutritionists and behavioural specialists. |
| **Behaviour** | **Head-bobbing**, a stereotypy signalling severe mental distress. **Killed the Math's chief swamiji in 2017**, slamming him repeatedly against a wall. **Attacked another man in 2022.** | Vantara reports emotional stabilisation through social contact. |

## Timeline

- **1992** She arrives at Swastishree Jinsen Bhattarak Pattacharya Mahaswami
  Sansthan, Nandani village, Shirol taluka, Kolhapur district. She is 3.
- **2017** She kills the Math's chief swamiji.
- **2022** She attacks another man. Video later surfaces.
- **27 Dec 2024 and 3 June 2025** The Supreme Court appointed High-Powered
  Committee (HPC) orders her transferred to Vantara, after PETA India complaints,
  multiple inspections and veterinary assessments.
- **The Math challenges it** in the Bombay High Court under **Article 25**,
  freedom of religion, arguing she is central to its religious activity.
- **16 July 2025** Bombay HC (Justices Revati Mohite Dere and Neela Gokhale)
  dismisses the petition and calls Vantara a **"godsent facility for the long
  suffering elephant"**. Transfer ordered within two weeks. Reported framing: an
  elephant's right to a quality life prevails over her use for religious rites.
- **28 July 2025** Supreme Court upholds it.
- **30 July 2025** She reaches Vantara's Radhe Krishna Temple Elephant Welfare
  Trust, Moti Khavdi, Jamnagar.
- **Within days** #BoycottJio begins, led by former MP **Raju Shetti**. Over
  **150,000 mobile users** across Kolhapur, Sangli and Satara port out of Jio in
  **four days**. A local telecom store gives a free elephant mug to every
  customer who switches.
- **CM Devendra Fadnavis** backs the return. Says Vantara is willing to join the
  state's petition.
- **9 Sep 2026** The HPC passes a reasoned order allowing her return, after
  hearing parties, reviewing reports and inspecting the prepared Nandani facility.
- **11 Sep 2026** She returns after 409 days. Thousands at midnight. The Math
  head weeps.

## The conditions she came back under

- **She cannot be used for processions, or for any other purpose.**
- Round-the-clock care.
- A **medical examination every fortnight**, with Vantara vets travelling to
  Kolhapur to conduct it and reporting to the authorities.
- Vantara upgraded the Nandani enclosure, and says further upgrades are blocked
  by **town planning constraints**: a road running through the Trust's land
  limits how much can be developed. Vantara says it remains available to upgrade
  further if directed.

## What is contested, and by whom

This is the part that decides whether the reel is honest.

**PETA India is a party to the case, not a neutral observer.** They opposed her
return and documented it as a failure: hobbled and shackled by two legs, amid
fireworks, screaming crowds and horns, pulled off the transport vehicle by her
tail into a crush of onlookers, and returned to solitary confinement in the same
shed where the 2017 and 2022 attacks happened.

**Vantara's own record is under scrutiny, and the file is sealed.**
- **Aug 2025** The Supreme Court ordered an **SIT** under retired Justice **Jasti
  Chelameswar** into alleged illegal wildlife transfers and unlawful captivity of
  elephants at Vantara.
- **March 2026** Süddeutsche Zeitung estimated roughly **39,000 animals** at
  Vantara, most acquired in the preceding year.
- **2024** Himal reported that the captive status of sub-adult elephants sent
  from Arunachal Pradesh could not be established, for want of DNA testing.
- **Sept 2026** The SIT filed its findings in a **sealed cover**. The Court gave
  Vantara a clean chit and ordered the **25,000-page report re-sealed and kept
  confidential**. Mongabay reported a global biodiversity assessment that
  countered the clean chit.

So: the Nandani conditions are documented by independent vets, HPC inspections
and two courts. The Vantara conditions come mostly from Vantara's own
communications, and the one independent investigation into them is sealed. Say
that out loud in the reel. It is the most defensible line available and nobody
else is saying it.

## The kicker for the reel

The city boycotted Jio, ported 150,000 numbers and fought for 409 days to bring
their temple elephant home. The order that brought her home **bans her from
ever appearing in a procession again**.

So the question to end on, unanswered: agar woh kabhi procession mein nahi aa
sakti, toh Kolhapur ne exactly kya jeeta?
