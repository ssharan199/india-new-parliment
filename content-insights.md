## The Divya Mittal reel: what actually failed

It did not fail on quality. 67 likes on 3,036 views is a 2.21% like rate, the
third highest on the whole account and better than the underground village reel
that did 619K. Everyone who watched it responded well.

It failed on **distribution**. It never escaped the first test batch. Three
causes, in order of size:

1. **Thumbnail and first frame.** Look at the grid. Every reel above 100K opens
   on a rendered 3D object with depth and light: a lit cruise ship, the Antilia
   tower, a cave room, a burning fuselage. The Mirzapur reel opens on a flat
   blue and yellow text logo. It is the only 2D frame in a grid of 3D renders,
   and it is the only reel under 15K. At 3K followers there is no owned audience
   to force a floor, so the opening frame is the entire distribution decision.

2. **Audience mismatch.** This account's viewers are 13 to 34 year old Indian
   men who came for scale, money, engineering and "inside the thing". A story
   about an IAS officer belongs to the UPSC-motivation community, which is a
   different graph entirely. Instagram had nobody to send it to.

3. **Unrenderable subject.** The account's edge is 3D CGI. A person's career
   arc has no object to model, so the format's only advantage is unused. The
   video becomes talking-head or stock footage, competing in a category where
   this account has no ranking history.

## What works

- **Scale plus a cutaway of something famous.** Cruise 7M, Antilia 952K,
  underground village 619K. The pattern is: a thing everyone knows exists, that
  nobody has seen inside, revealed in 3D. The two 3%-like-rate reels are both
  this exact shape.
- **Direct comparison.** IMAX vs Dune vs Normal did 367K with a 0.44% share
  rate, near the top of the account. Comparisons get sent to a friend to settle
  an argument. Shares are the reach multiplier here.
- **Hindi title on a spectacle topic.** ज़मीन के अंदर बना डाला गाँव pulled 3,000
  shares, the second highest share count on the account and the highest share
  rate at 0.48%, off 619K views. Hindi on a visual topic outperforms; Hindi on a
  grim topic (Jharia, 49K) does not rescue it.
- **Superlative naming.** World's Biggest, 17,000 crore, Most Dangerous, Most
  Luxurious. The number or the superlative is doing the click.

## What does not work

- **Nature and science trivia.** Anglerfish 115K at a 0.58% like rate, whale
  vomit 14,766. Views without affection. This audience is not a nature-doc
  audience, and these two reels drag the account's topical signal sideways.
- **Grim and depressing subjects.** Jharia 49K (0.47% likes), Mission Failed
  43K. There is nothing aspirational to send onward. Compare Most Luxurious Jail
  at only 15K views but a 0.33% share rate: the luxury framing earns shares even
  when reach is small.
- **Person-led stories.** The two lowest-context reels on people are the
  Divya Mittal reel (3K) and @bittu_tabahi (81K, and that one borrowed an
  existing creator's audience via the tag, so it is not a repeatable win).
- **Vague titles.** "Mission Failed..." and "Real Mirzapur Story of..." name no
  entity and no number. Every winner names both.

## Two structural problems bigger than any single reel

**1. Output is declining, not just uneven.** July produced 7M, 952K and 367K.
The Aug 13 to Sep 12 window's own posts total roughly 1.04M across eight reels,
and 619K of that is one reel. The 4.7M headline number is mostly trailing views
on the July cruise reel, not new performance. The last month's new content is
roughly one seventh of July's.

**2. 4.7M views converted to 3,028 followers.** That is the worst number in the
export. A 7M-view reel should have built an audience and did not, because
consecutive reels give a viewer no reason to expect the next one: anglerfish,
whale vomit, Modi's security, a jail, a plane crash, Mirzapur. No recurring
format, no series name, no visual signature beyond "it is 3D". Views are being
rented, not banked. With 8.7K shares against only 6.1K saves on 4.7M views
(0.13% save rate), people are passing content along and not keeping it.

## What to do

1. **Kill the flat thumbnail rule.** Frame one of every reel is a lit 3D render
   with depth. No text-card openers, ever.
2. **Run one repeatable series.** "Inside [famous thing]" is already proven
   three times on this account. Name it, use a consistent opening move (exterior
   to cutaway), and post it weekly so the format becomes the reason to follow.
3. **Cadence over variety.** Eight reels in 30 days across seven unrelated
   topics is why every post starts cold. Two series, alternating, beats eight
   one-offs.
4. **Rescue the Divya Mittal story if the subject matters.** The only version
   that fits this account is object-led, for example the district itself
   rendered in 3D, or the specific system she changed shown as a mechanism, with
   the person as narration rather than as the subject. If it cannot be rendered,
   it does not belong on this account.
5. **Ask one question in the caption and pin it.** 302 comments on 4.7M views is
   0.006%. Comments are the one engagement signal this account has essentially
   zero of, and it is free to fix.
6. **Build for shares, not likes.** The three highest share rates (0.48%, 0.44%,
   0.33%) came from comparison and luxury framings. Shares are what carried the
   underground village reel to 619K.
