# Sansad Bhavan — voiceover script (Hinglish)

~85 seconds. Shot cues map to the camera presets in the viewer, so the reel can
be cut straight against the model.

---

**[0:00 · Aerial]**
Aap apna ghar square banate ho. Rectangle banate ho.
Toh India ka naya Sansad Bhavan **triangle** kyun hai?
Iske peeche teen wajah hain — aur teeno chalaak hain.

**[0:09 · Facade]**
Pehli — **structure**. Triangle engineering ki sabse stable shape hai.
Push it, twist it, shake it — apna aakaar nahi chhodta.
Isi liye ye building Delhi ke seismic zone ke liye design ki gayi hai.

**[0:20 · Six dwars]**
Doosri — **symbolism**. Teen kone, teen ceremonial dwar:
**Gyan**, **Shakti**, aur **Karma** — gyaan, shakti, aur kartavya.
Par asli maza yahan hai: darwaaze chhe hain, aur har ek ka apna jaanwar hai.

**[0:32 · Six dwars, slow orbit]**
**Gaja Dwar** — haathi. Buddhi aur samriddhi.
**Ashwa Dwar** — ghoda. Taakat aur himmat.
**Garuda Dwar** — Vishnu ka vaahan. Shakti aur dharma.
Aur teen public gates — **Makara**, ek aadha-machhli jeev, "unity in diversity";
**Shardula**, sher aur baagh ka mishran, "power of the people";
aur **Hamsa** — hans, moksha ka prateek.
Chhe darwaaze, chhe kahaniyan.

**[0:52 · Lok Sabha]**
Andar jao — aur har kamra ek symbol pehanta hai.
**Lok Sabha**: theme hai **mor**, hamara rashtriya pakshi.
Chhat par mor ke pankh, farsh par hari carpet, aur 888 seats.

**[1:02 · Rajya Sabha]**
**Rajya Sabha**: theme hai **kamal**, rashtriya phool.
Laal carpet, kamal ki pankhudiyan chhat par, 384 seats.
Dono milkar joint session mein 1,272 log baith sakte hain.

**[1:12 · Courtyard]**
Aur beech mein? Khula aasman, aur ek **bargad ka ped** — rashtriya vriksh.

**[1:18 · Emblem]**
Sabse upar, chhat par — **Ashoka ka Sinh Stambh**.
Chaar sher, chaar disha, ek desh.

**[1:24 · Aerial pull-back]**
Toh ye sirf ek shape nahi hai.
Ye science hai, culture hai, aur smart design — teeno ek saath.

## Notes on the original draft

Three things I changed rather than repeated, because they didn't hold up:

- **"Lagbhag earthquake-proof"** → softened to "seismic zone ke liye design ki
  gayi hai". A triangle is a stiff *truss* shape, but a triangular floor plan
  doesn't make a building earthquake-proof — that comes from the structural
  system, not the outline. The claim is the kind that gets picked apart in
  comments.
- **"Teen gates … jo Trimurti ko darshate hain"** → kept the three ceremonial
  gates and their Gyan/Shakti/Karma meanings, dropped the Trimurti link. My
  sources describe the gates as knowledge, strength and duty; none of them
  connect them to the Trimurti. Also worth knowing: there are **six** doors in
  total, not three — which is better material anyway, so the script uses it.
- **"40 se zyada MPs ke office"** → replaced with the seat counts. The figure I
  can source is **92 minister offices** plus 6 committee rooms, not 40. If you
  want the office line back, use 92.

One naming point: the courtyard tree is a **bargad (banyan)**, the national
tree — not an ashoka tree. Ashoka appears separately, as the **Lion Capital** on
the roof. The script keeps them as two distinct beats.

Sources: NextIAS on the six entrances; TNPSC Thervu Pettagam on the chambers;
Business Today on the dwars. See README.
