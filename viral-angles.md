# @binarybizz: 10 viral angles

Filter applied: EXTREME + REAL + VISUALLY UNDERSTANDABLE + ONE BIG QUESTION,
plus two hard rules of my own:

- **Zero setup.** The viewer must already know the thing exists. No "who is X".
- **The money shot must be unfilmable.** If a news channel has the footage, you
  have no edge. If the shot only exists as a render, the reel has to be yours.

Every number below needs verifying before publish. Comparison reels die in the
comments on a wrong figure.

---

## 1. Kailasa Temple, Ellora
**Question:** Ek hi chattan se, upar se neeche, mandir kaise kaat sakte ho? Ek
galti aur poora mandir barbaad.
**Money shot:** Subtractive animation. Start with the solid basalt hill, then
remove rock in layers until the temple emerges, top down. Roughly 200,000 tonnes
carried out by hand, 8th century, no possibility of undo.
**Why it fits:** This is the single best 3D subject in India and nobody can film
it, because the shot is the rock that is no longer there. Maps to your Antilia
cutaway win.
**Difficulty:** Medium. One hero mesh plus a boolean reveal sequence.

## 2. Chenab Rail Bridge
**Question:** Gorge ke beech mein zameen hi nahi hai, toh arch kis par banaya?
**Money shot:** The cable crane strung across the gorge, two half arches
cantilevered out from opposite cliffs, meeting in mid air with nothing
underneath. Then pull back to show the Eiffel Tower standing inside the gorge
and still being shorter (359 m rail level vs 330 m).
**Why it fits:** Scale plus India pride. Your highest share rates came from
comparison framings, and this is a comparison with a landmark everyone knows.
**Difficulty:** Medium. The arch is repeating geometry, the gorge is terrain.

## 3. Where India's gold actually is
**Question:** India ka saara sona kitni jagah leta hai? And why is a large part
of it sitting in a vault in London?
**Money shot:** The scale shock. Around 880 tonnes of gold is a cube of roughly
**3.6 metres per side**. Put that cube in a normal Indian bedroom and it does not
fill it. Then cut to the split: RBI vaults in Mumbai and Nagpur versus the Bank
of England.
**Why it fits:** Money and power is your proven lane (Antilia, 952K, 3.00% like
rate). The cube is a one second idea that people screenshot.
**Difficulty:** Low. Cubes, a room, a vault. Highest reward per hour of work here.

## 4. Inside Jamnagar
**Question:** Antilia toh ghar hai. Ambani ki asli machine kya hai?
**Money shot:** Cutaway of the world's largest refining complex. One pipe of
crude in, twenty plus products out, traced as colour coded flows through the
distillation column. Built on barren salt land, now with a mango orchard inside
the fence.
**Why it fits:** This is the repeat move. Your audience already responded to this
exact entity at 3.00% likes. Do not waste a proven entity on one reel.
**Difficulty:** Medium high. Lots of pipe geometry, but pipes are procedural.

## 5. Kola Superdeep Borehole
**Question:** Chaand par pahunch gaye, par zameen mein 12 km se aage kyun nahi
ja paaye?
**Money shot:** Earth in cross section with the borehole drawn to true scale, a
line thinner than a hair, 0.19% of the way to the centre. Then the reveal: at
the bottom it hit about 180 C, the rock started behaving like plastic, and they
welded the hole shut.
**Why it fits:** Pure scale humiliation, which is what your cruise reel did at 7M.
**Difficulty:** Low. Cross section plus a zoom. Cheap and very strong.

## 6. How a submarine breathes
**Question:** Teen mahine paani ke andar. Saans kahan se, paani kahan se?
**Money shot:** Full length cutaway of a nuclear submarine, then zoom into the
three systems that keep humans alive: electrolysis splitting seawater for oxygen,
amine scrubbers pulling CO2 out of the air, distillation making drinking water.
The punchline: air is not the limit, food is.
**Why it fits:** Cutaway of a famous object is literally your 3% formula, and the
defence topic is centred on your 13 to 34 male audience.
**Difficulty:** Medium. Hull plus four labelled machine bays.

## 7. Why the Taj Mahal's minarets lean outward
**Question:** Chaaron minaar seedhe nahi hain. Jaan boojh kar tedhe banaye gaye?
**Money shot:** Exaggerate the tilt, then run an earthquake simulation twice:
straight minarets falling onto the dome, tilted minarets falling away from it.
Then go under the ground to the well foundation sunk beside the Yamuna.
**Why it fits:** Maximum zero setup in India. Also honest fuel for comments,
because the earthquake explanation is the popular one and scholars also argue
for perspective correction and natural settling. Say both. Let them fight.
**Difficulty:** Medium. Reuse the Parliament repo's stone texture generators.

## 8. INS Vikrant's ramp vs America's catapult
**Question:** India ke carrier par ramp hai, America ke par nahi. Kyun?
**Money shot:** Same jet, two decks, side by side, launched in sync. Ski jump
trades payload for simplicity, catapult trades complexity for full fuel and full
weapons load. Show the fuel and bomb load each one gives up.
**Why it fits:** This is your IMAX vs Dune structure (367K, 0.44% share rate)
pointed at a subject your audience argues about for free.
**Difficulty:** Medium. Two decks, one jet, physics you can fake convincingly.

## 9. Chandrayaan 2 vs Chandrayaan 3
**Question:** Same ISRO, same rocket, same chaand. Ek crash, ek safe. Kya badla?
**Money shot:** Both descent trajectories overlaid on one screen, the exact point
where 2 diverged, then the four design changes on the lander itself: stronger
legs, more fuel margin, a wider target zone, and one engine removed.
**Why it fits:** Comparison plus national story plus a genuine answer. ISRO needs
no introduction to anyone in your audience.
**Difficulty:** Medium. The lander is the only detailed mesh needed.

## 10. Burj Khalifa's concrete
**Question:** 50 degree garmi mein, 600 metre upar, liquid concrete raaste mein
set kaise nahi hua?
**Money shot:** Follow one batch of concrete from the mixer to floor 150. Ice in
the mix, poured only at night, pumped at pressures that had never been used
before, up a pipe inside the core. Cut to the Y shaped buttressed core and show
why that shape lets the tower stand in wind.
**Why it fits:** The most famous extreme building on earth, and you have not
touched it. Safe, factual, and the ice detail is the shareable bit.
**Difficulty:** Low to medium. Tower massing plus a pipe and a pump.

## Picking order

If the goal is the first win with least build time: **3 (gold cube)**, then
**5 (Kola)**. Both are cheap geometry with a hard scale payoff.

If the goal is the reel that defines the account: **1 (Kailasa)**. It is the one
subject on this list where 3D is not a substitute for footage, it is the only
possible way to show the thing at all.

If the goal is repeating a proven win: **4 (Jamnagar)**, because your audience
already told you they care about Ambani at a 3.00% like rate.
